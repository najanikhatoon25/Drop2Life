# 💧 DropBack – Don't Waste the Last Drop

## About the Project

**DropBack** is a leftover water reuse project that aims to stop clean drinking water from being unnecessarily wasted.

The idea is simple: when some water is left in a bottle or glass, instead of throwing it away, we collect it and give it a **second purpose**.

## 🚨 Problem We Are Solving

* People often throw leftover drinking water into dustbins, drains, or on the ground.
* There is no simple and dedicated system for collecting leftover water.
* People think a small amount of leftover water does not make a difference.
* At schools, colleges, offices, events, and public places, these small amounts can collectively become significant.
* The opportunity to reuse already available water for suitable non-drinking purposes is often missed.

## 💡 Our Idea

We are converting a **waste point into a reuse point**.

**Bottle/Glass → DropBack Reuse Point → Collection → Second Use**

The collected water can be used for:

* 🌱 Watering plants and gardens
* 🧹 Cleaning and mopping
* 🚻 Suitable non-drinking uses such as toilet flushing
* 🌿 Outdoor cleaning

## 🌱 Our Future Ideas

* 💧 Display showing **how many litres were saved**
* 📊 Track daily and monthly water recovery
* 🏆 Reward system for schools/classes or communities
* 📱 Future mobile dashboard
* 🔲 QR-based participation system
* 📈 Expand from one campus to offices, parks, events, and other public places

## 🎯 Our Core Message

> **Every leftover drop deserves a second purpose.**

**Don't waste the last drop. Give it another purpose.**
